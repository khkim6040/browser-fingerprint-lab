DIRECTThe browser reports the real value as-is.

UNAVAILABLEWithheld by this browser, or never available to any page. Half the exhibit.

A website does not need your camera, microphone or location to tell your machine apart from the next one. Dozens of small, permission-free signals — the GPU's name, how its shaders round, which codecs decode in hardware, how the audio pipeline compresses a tone — add up to a description specific enough to recognise a device again.

Never read by this page: files, browsing history, passwords, clipboard, camera or microphone input, precise location, MAC address, hardware serials.
